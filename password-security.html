Your password is the first line of defense in protecting your personal information online.

WHAT MAKES A STRONG PASSWORD?

  • ✔ At least 15 characters long
  • ✔ Contains uppercase letters
  • ✔ Contains lowercase letters
  • ✔ Includes numbers
  • ✔ Uses special characters
  • ✔ Different for every account

PASSWORD EXAMPLE

Weak Password: password123

Stronger Example: River!Cloud7Moon#Tree

IMPORTANT PASSWORD TIPS

  • Never share your password with others.
  • Avoid using birthdays and personal information.
  • Do not reuse the same password.
  • Use multi-factor authentication when available.
  • Change compromised passwords immediately.

REMEMBER: Think before you share. Protect your password and protect your digital identity!
